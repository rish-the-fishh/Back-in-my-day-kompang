# Back-in-my-day-kompang

This was our submission for the Spatial Hack AI hackathon by Apple. Our team built an app that allowed you interact with different NPCs from different time periods in Singapore and learn cultural practices from them that were at risk of dying. In my case, I was tasked to create a NPC named Uncle Yusof who would teach the user how to play the kompang, a malay drum that is played during special occasions such as weddings, in the form of a rhythm game. The user must try to follow the rhythm of the beat that Uncle asks them to play, similar to a rhythm game.
